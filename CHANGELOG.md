## [1.1.0] - 2026-05-17

### Added

- Support for 11 new countries: United Arab Emirates (`AE`), Argentina (`AR`), Chile (`CL`),
  Colombia (`CO`), South Korea (`KR`), Norway (`NO`), New Zealand (`NZ`), Russia (`RU`),
  Saudi Arabia (`SA`), Sweden (`SE`), and South Africa (`ZA`)
- New `CountryCode` and `CurrencyCode` exported types for stricter ISO definitions
- Aliases `fromCents` and `fromInt` for the `fromMinorUnits` creation function

## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-12

First stable version for production use.
